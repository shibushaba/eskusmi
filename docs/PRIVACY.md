# Privacy Policy — eskusmi

Last updated: 2026-08-21

eskusmi is a local desktop app. It is designed to work on your private LAN without requiring a cloud account.

## What eskusmi stores

- A display name / presence profile you choose
- App preferences (for example status and launch-at-login)
- Data is stored locally on your device (via the app’s local store)

## What eskusmi sends on the network

- Presence and ping messages to other eskusmi devices on the **same local network**
- Traffic stays on your LAN; the app does not require an eskusmi cloud backend

## What we do not collect

- No mandatory account signup
- No advertising trackers in the desktop app
- No sale of personal data

## Downloads and the website

The download page and GitHub Releases may use normal hosting/CDN logs (for example GitHub or Vercel request logs). Those are controlled by those providers’ policies.
